## DOCUMENT 1: UNIVERSAL FINDER SERVICE AGREEMENT
**Use for: Wisconsin, Texas, Pennsylvania, Illinois (non-registration states)**

```
UNCLAIMED PROPERTY FINDER SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on [DATE] between:

FINDER: [YOUR NAME], ZGroup LLC
Address: [YOUR ADDRESS]
Email: [YOUR EMAIL]   Phone: [YOUR PHONE]

and

CLAIMANT: [CLAIMANT NAME]
Address: [CLAIMANT ADDRESS]
Email: [CLAIMANT EMAIL]   Phone: [CLAIMANT PHONE]

PROPERTY DETAILS:
State: Wisconsin
Property ID: [PROPERTY ID]
Approximate Amount: $[AMOUNT]
Property Type: [TYPE]

TERMS:

1. SERVICES. The Finder will assist the Claimant in locating and
   claiming unclaimed property held by the State of Wisconsin,
   including providing property details, guidance on completing
   claim forms, and email support during the process.

2. CLAIMANT'S RIGHT TO CLAIM DIRECTLY. The Claimant acknowledges
   they may claim this property directly from the state at NO COST:
   revenue.wi.gov/Pages/UnclaimedProperty  |  (608) 267-7977

3. FINDER'S FEE. If the Claimant successfully receives payment from
   the state, the Claimant agrees to pay the Finder [10/15/20]% of
   the amount received. This fee is contingent and voluntary; no
   fee is owed unless and until the Claimant is paid.

4. PAYMENT. Fee due within 30 days of the Claimant receiving payment
   from the state. Methods: [Venmo / PayPal / Zelle / Check].

5. NO UPFRONT COST. The Claimant pays nothing in advance and bears
   no risk; the Finder is compensated only on success.

6. NO GUARANTEE. The Finder does not guarantee the property will be
   released, any specific amount, or any timeline.

7. CANCELLATION. Either party may cancel at any time without penalty.

CLAIMANT ACKNOWLEDGMENT:
I understand I can claim this property for FREE directly from the
state. I am choosing to work with the Finder for assistance and
convenience, and I agree to the contingent fee only if I am paid.

CLAIMANT SIGNATURE: ____________________  DATE: ________
CLAIMANT NAME (PRINTED): _______________________________
FINDER SIGNATURE: ______________________  DATE: ________

## DOCUMENT 2: CALIFORNIA ASSET LOCATOR AGREEMENT
**Required for California — you MUST register with the State Controller first.**

Key California rules to build in:
- Register as an asset locator before soliciting.
- Fee is capped at 10% when the property has been held less than 24 months (verify current statute before relying on any higher figure for older property).
- A written, signed agreement is required before any fee is owed.
- The owner's free-claim right must be disclosed.

```
CALIFORNIA ASSET LOCATOR AGREEMENT

Asset Locator Registration #: [YOUR CA REGISTRATION NUMBER]

FINDER: [YOUR NAME], ZGroup LLC — Email/Phone: [___]
OWNER: [OWNER NAME] — Address/Email: [___]

PROPERTY: ID [___], Amount $[___], Type [___], State: California

1. REGISTRATION. Finder is registered with the California State
   Controller's Office as an asset locator (# above).

2. FEE LIMIT. Finder's fee is [X]% of the amount recovered, within
   the limits set by California law for property of this age.

3. FREE CLAIM RIGHT. Owner may claim directly at no cost:
   ucpi.sco.ca.gov/UCP  |  (800) 992-4647

4. WRITTEN AGREEMENT. This signed agreement is required before any
   fee is payable. A copy has been provided to the Owner.

5. CONTINGENT FEE. Payable only if the Owner receives funds, within
   30 days of receipt. No upfront cost.

6. NO GUARANTEE. No guarantee of success, amount, or timeline.

7. CANCELLATION. Either party may cancel at any time.

OWNER SIGNATURE: ____________________  DATE: ________
Copy provided to Owner on: ________
FINDER SIGNATURE: ___________________  DATE: ________
```

## DOCUMENT 4: FLORIDA FINDER AGREEMENT
**Florida: 5-day cancellation right; finder agreements restricted on recently-reported property.**

```
FLORIDA UNCLAIMED PROPERTY FINDER AGREEMENT

FINDER: [YOUR NAME], ZGroup LLC — [contact]
CLAIMANT: [NAME] — [contact]
PROPERTY: ID [___], Amount $[___], Type [___], Reported [YEAR]

1. FIVE-DAY CANCELLATION. Claimant may cancel within 5 days of
   signing with no penalty. To cancel, email [YOUR EMAIL] stating
   "I cancel this agreement." Deadline: [DATE + 5 DAYS].

2. PROPERTY-AGE RULE. Florida restricts finder agreements on
   property recently reported to the state. This property was
   reported in [YEAR] and qualifies. (Verify current holding period.)

3. FEE LIMIT. Up to 20% for qualifying property. Agreed: [X]%.

4. FREE CLAIM RIGHT. Claimant may claim directly at no cost:
   fltreasurehunt.gov  |  (888) 258-2253

5. CONTINGENT FEE. Payable only if the Claimant is paid, within
   30 days of receipt. No upfront cost.

6. NO GUARANTEE. No guarantee of approval, amount, or timeline.

NOTICE: You have 5 DAYS to cancel. You can claim for FREE from the
state. The fee applies only if you receive payment.

CLAIMANT SIGNATURE: ____________________  DATE: ________
CANCELLATION PERIOD ENDS: [DATE + 5 DAYS]
FINDER SIGNATURE: ______________________  DATE: ________

[ ] Death certificate
[ ] Letters testamentary / Letters of administration
[ ] Court order appointing representative
[ ] Affidavit of heirship (if no formal probate)

1. AUTHORITY. Representative confirms legal authority to act for
   the estate regarding unclaimed property.

2. COMPLEXITY FEE. Due to probate, documentation, and multi-heir
   coordination, the fee is [20-25]% of the amount recovered.

3. FREE CLAIM RIGHT. The estate may claim directly at no cost:
   [STATE WEBSITE]  |  [STATE PHONE]

4. CONTINGENT FEE. Payable only if the estate receives funds,
   within 30 days of receipt. No upfront cost.

5. MULTIPLE BENEFICIARIES. Representative agrees to pay the fee
   from estate funds before distribution, or coordinate payment
   among beneficiaries.

6. NO GUARANTEE. No guarantee of approval, amount, or timeline.

REPRESENTATIVE SIGNATURE: ____________________  DATE: ________
FINDER SIGNATURE: ____________________________  DATE: ________

## RED FLAGS — DO NOT PROCEED

Do not work with anyone who:
- Refuses to sign an agreement
- Wants to hide the claim from the state
- Asks you to misrepresent anything on forms
- Is trying to claim property that isn't theirs
- Pressures you for upfront payment from them, or
- Pressures you to skip the free-claim disclosure

If something feels wrong, walk away. In this business, honesty and compliance are also your best protection.
