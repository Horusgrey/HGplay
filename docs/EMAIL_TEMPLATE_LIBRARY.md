# EMAIL TEMPLATE LIBRARY
## Battle-Tested Copy for Maximum Response Rates

The difference between 1% and 4% response rates is the copy. These templates are built on what actually works in cold outreach: short, specific, low-pressure, and credible.

---

## THE CORE PRINCIPLE

People ignore emails that feel like scams. The unclaimed-property space is full of bad actors, so your #1 job is to **sound legitimate and low-pressure**. Every template below does three things:

1. Leads with THEIR money (specific amount, specific source)
2. Tells them they can claim it FREE without you
3. Makes the fee optional and framed as convenience

This is also what keeps you ethical. The honesty *is* the conversion strategy.

## TEMPLATE A — HIGH VALUE ($10,000+)

**For your ultra-tier prospects (Vasser, Gao, etc.)**

These deserve a different tone: more formal, more "this is real," and ideally a phone follow-up.

**Subject lines (rotate to test):**
- `[First Name], unclaimed funds in your name — Wisconsin DOR`
- `Regarding $[Amount] held by the State of Wisconsin`
- `[First Name] [Last Name] — verified unclaimed property`

**Body:**
```
Hi [First Name],

I research unclaimed property records, and your name is attached
to a sizable account currently held by the Wisconsin Department of
Revenue — originally from [Holder/Source].

The amount is significant, so I wanted to reach out personally
rather than send a form letter.

A few things you should know up front:

• This is YOUR money. The state is holding it for you.
• You can claim it yourself for free at:
  revenue.wi.gov/Pages/UnclaimedProperty
• You do not need me or anyone else to get it.

I help people who'd rather not deal with the paperwork and
back-and-forth with the state. If you'd like that help, I work
on a contingency basis — I only get paid if you actually receive
your money, and we agree on the amount in writing first.

If you'd rather do it yourself, I'm genuinely glad to point you
in the right direction at no charge.

Either way, I'd recommend claiming it soon.

Would you like me to send you the details?

[Your Name]
[Your Phone]
[Your Email]
ZGroup LLC · Wisconsin
```

**Why it works:** Acknowledges the size, leads with legitimacy, repeats "free" twice, no pressure, ends with a low-commitment yes/no question.

## FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE

Most responses come from follow-ups, not the first email. Send these 7 days apart, max 3 total.

```
No rush, but these do get returned to the state's general fund
```

```
If it's useful, reply and I'll send the exact steps. If not, no

revenue.wi.gov/Pages/UnclaimedProperty

[Your Name]
```

### Follow-Up #3 (Day 21) — The Closer
```
Hi [First Name],

Last one, I promise. Your $[Amount] is still sitting unclaimed
with Wisconsin.

Whether you use me or not, please grab it — it's yours:
revenue.wi.gov/Pages/UnclaimedProperty

[Your Name]
```

**Why three works:** Diminishing pressure + escalating "this is your last chance to hear from me" makes people act. Always reaffirming the free option keeps you clean.

## SUBJECT LINE TESTING MATRIX

Track open rates and rotate. Test these structures:

| Structure | Example | Best For |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| Name + benefit | `[Name], unclaimed money in Wisconsin` | Standard |
| Dollar amount | `Found $1,250 in your name` | Mid-value |
| Authority | `Regarding funds held by Wisconsin DOR` | High-value |
| Curiosity | `Did you know about this, [Name]?` | A/B test |
| Direct | `Unclaimed property — [Name] [Last]` | Brief |

**Rule:** Never use ALL CAPS, never use "FREE MONEY," never use excessive punctuation (!!!). These trip spam filters and scam radar.

---

## DELIVERABILITY RULES (Don't Land in Spam)

1. **Volume:** Stay under 50 emails/day from a personal Gmail. Scale with a dedicated domain + warmup.
2. **No spam-trigger words:** Avoid "free money," "guaranteed," "act now," "winner," "$$$."
3. **Plain text beats HTML** for this use case — looks personal, not marketing.
4. **One link maximum** (the state website).
5. **Real signature** with your actual name, business, contact.
6. **Ask recipients to reply** — replies tell Gmail you're legitimate.
7. **Monitor your sender reputation** — if open rates crater, pause and warm up.

## WHAT NOT TO DO

❌ Don't claim you're from the government
❌ Don't say "you must act now or lose it" (false urgency)
❌ Don't hide the free option
❌ Don't ask for SSN, bank details, or payment upfront
❌ Don't use fake scarcity or countdown timers
❌ Don't send from a no-reply address
❌ Don't buy email lists — use skip-traced contacts only

Every one of these is both an ethics violation AND a conversion killer. Legitimate = profitable here.
