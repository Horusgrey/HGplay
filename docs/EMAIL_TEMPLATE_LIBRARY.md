# EMAIL / LETTER TEMPLATE LIBRARY
## Copy for Outreach — Gratuity-First, Honest, Low-Pressure

The difference between a scam-sounding letter and one people trust is the copy.
These templates are built on one idea: **lead with their money, tell them the
free option first, and never require a fee.**

---

## THE CORE PRINCIPLE

People ignore (or report) outreach that feels like a scam. Your #1 job is to
**sound legitimate and low-pressure**. Every template below does three things:

1. Leads with THEIR money (specific amount, specific source)
2. Tells them they can claim it FREE without you — first, not as a footnote
3. Treats any payment as optional and owner-chosen, never a quoted percentage

This is also what keeps the model legal. Wisconsin caps a *contingency fee
agreement* at 10% and voids it if signed before 24 months of state custody
(`compliance.py`). A **gratuity** — no quoted number, no signed fee schedule,
offered only after the person already has their money — isn't that kind of
agreement at all. The moment a specific percentage gets printed in a letter or
email, it stops being a gratuity and becomes the fee the statute caps. So:

> **Never print a percentage in outreach copy.** `letter_generator.py`'s
> GRATUITY mode (the default, and the one that should carry most of the
> volume) says a thank-you "of your own choosing" is appreciated and never
> required — nothing else. Only CONTRACT mode states a number, and it's
> always pulled from `compliance.compliant_fee_pct()` (10%, hard-capped),
> never typed by hand.

---

## MODE A — GRATUITY (default; most volume should go here)

Matches `letter_generator.letter_text(prospect, mode="GRATUITY")` almost
word for word. This is the plain-text version for email/SMS use; the PDF
letter is the primary surface.

**Subject lines:**
- `[First Name], unclaimed money in your name — Wisconsin`
- `Found $[Amount] in your name (Wisconsin)`
- `Unclaimed property for [First Name] [Last Name]`

**Body:**
```
Hi [First Name],

I came across your name while researching Wisconsin unclaimed-property
records — the state is holding about $[Amount] that belongs to you,
originally from [Holder/Source].

First, the honest part: this is your money, and you can claim it
yourself for free, directly from the state, at
revenue.wi.gov/Pages/UnclaimedProperty or by calling (608) 267-7977.
You do not need me or anyone else to get it.

I'm a private individual, not the State of Wisconsin or any
government agency. If the paperwork feels like a hassle, I'm glad to
walk you through it at no cost. If it works out and you'd like to
send a thank-you of your own choosing afterward, that's always
appreciated — but never expected and never required.

Either way, I hope you get what's yours.

[Your Name]
[Your Phone] · [Your Email]
ZGroup LLC · Wisconsin
```

**Why it works:** No number anywhere. "That's the honest part" and repeating
"free" disarms scam suspicion. The ask, if any, is made only after they're
already paid — never a condition of help.

## MODE B — CONTRACT (only after verified 24-month custody + human review)

Matches `letter_generator.letter_text(prospect, mode="CONTRACT")`. Only ever
generated by code that has already run `compliance.assert_agreement_allowed()`
— never hand-typed, never offered on an unverified or under-24-month record.

**Body (fee line only — rest of letter is identical to Mode A through the
"not a government agency" line):**
```
If you'd rather I handle the paperwork and filing for you, I offer
that on a simple contingency basis: a 10% fee (the maximum Wisconsin
allows), agreed in writing first, and owed only if you're actually
paid — no upfront cost, cancel anytime. I've enclosed a one-page
agreement you can read over. There's no obligation, and claiming it
yourself for free is always an option.
```

The "10%" above is illustrative of the current cap — if `WI_FEE_CAP` in
`compliance.py` is ever updated after re-verifying guidance, this doc should
be regenerated from the code, not hand-edited to a different number.

## FOLLOW-UP

`followup_engine.py` enforces **one follow-up, 21 days after the first
letter, then stop** (`FOLLOWUP_DAYS` / `MAX_LETTERS`) — not the 3-touch
cadence an unrestricted sequence might use. One nudge, then silence unless
they reply.

```
Hi [First Name],

Just floating this back to the top of your inbox — there's still
$[Amount] in unclaimed property in your name with Wisconsin.

Free to claim yourself: revenue.wi.gov/Pages/UnclaimedProperty
Or reply and I'm glad to help, same terms as before.

[Your Name]
```

## RESPONSE TEMPLATES

### "INTERESTED / Yes, help me" — GRATUITY path (default)
```
Great — happy to help, [First Name].

Here's how it works:

1. You file the claim with Wisconsin — I'll give you the exact forms
   and walk you through them, or help you prepare everything to sign.
2. The state processes it (usually 60–90 days) and sends YOU the
   check directly.
3. That's it. If you'd like to say thanks afterward, that's always
   welcome and entirely up to you — no amount expected.

To get started, can you confirm:
- Your full name and any previous addresses in Wisconsin
- That [Source/Holder] sounds familiar to you

Talk soon,
[Your Name]
```

### "Is this a scam?"
```
Totally fair question — you should be skeptical.

Unclaimed property is a real, government-run program. Verify yours
directly, right now, without me:

revenue.wi.gov/Pages/UnclaimedProperty

Search your name there. If your money shows up, it's real. You can
claim it yourself for free and never involve me at all — genuinely
fine either way. I only ever ask for anything after you've already
been paid, and never a set amount. If anyone in this space asks you
to pay before you receive your money, walk away.

[Your Name]
```

### "WRONG PERSON"
```
Thanks for letting me know, [First Name] — removing you from my list
now. Sorry for the interruption.

[Your Name]
```

### "ALREADY CLAIMED"
```
Great to hear — glad you got it sorted, [First Name]. Taking you off
my list. Take care!

[Your Name]
```

### "UNSUBSCRIBE / Stop"
```
Done — you won't hear from me again.

[Your Name]
```

These map directly to `reply_classifier.py`'s categories (INTERESTED,
WRONG_PERSON, ALREADY_CLAIMED, OPT_OUT) and the suppression it writes — an
opt-out here permanently sets `suppression_status` so no module can contact
that record again.

## DELIVERABILITY

1. Plain text over HTML — reads as personal, not marketing.
2. One link maximum (the state site).
3. Real signature, real name, real business.
4. Ask for a reply — replies build sender reputation.
5. Nothing here sends itself. Every message goes through `outbox.py`'s
   approve-to-send queue; there is no cron job, no "dailyAutomation," and no
   auto-categorize-and-reply step anywhere in this codebase. A human approves
   every send.

---

## WHAT NOT TO DO

❌ Don't claim you're from the government
❌ Don't say "you must act now or lose it" (false urgency)
❌ Don't hide the free option, ever
❌ Don't ask for SSN, bank details, or any payment upfront
❌ Don't quote a fee percentage in GRATUITY-mode copy
❌ Don't quote anything above `compliance.compliant_fee_pct()` in CONTRACT mode
❌ Don't send from a no-reply address
❌ Don't auto-send anything without a human approving it in `outbox.py`

Every one of these is both an ethics violation and, for the fee-related ones,
a live legal problem under WI Stat. ch. 177 Subch. XIII.
